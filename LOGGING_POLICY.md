# Logging Policy

Structured logs may contain timestamp, severity, correlation ID, safe event name, environment, organisation UUID, provider key, latency, status, and redacted error category. Never log passwords, sessions, access/refresh tokens, cookies, OTPs, PINs, API/webhook secrets, encryption keys, full bank details, raw KYC content, or unredacted provider payloads. Restrict access, define retention, monitor exports, preserve incident evidence, and test redaction.
