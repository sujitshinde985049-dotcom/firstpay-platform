# Incident Response

For provider/database outage, webhook backlog, credential leakage, unauthorised access, cross-tenant exposure, failed deployment, suspicious Super Admin activity, duplicate-debit concern, integrity issue, or Storage exposure: detect and classify; contain access/writes/routing; preserve logs and evidence; investigate correlation IDs and audit history; rotate affected credentials; recover through reviewed runbooks; validate tenant and financial integrity; communicate accurately with affected clients and advisers; and complete a blameless post-incident review with tracked remediation.

Cross-tenant exposure and duplicate-debit concerns are critical incidents requiring immediate containment and executive/security escalation.
