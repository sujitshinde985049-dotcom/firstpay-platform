# Merchant Onboarding

The workflow records business profile completion, private KYC documents, provider selection/application, internal and provider review, sandbox approval, production credential readiness, and live approval. PAN, bank, and identity data remain placeholders until encryption, legal basis, retention controls, and restricted access are approved.

Documents use the private `merchant-kyc` bucket under an organisation UUID prefix. Malware scanning is a blocking placeholder for production.
