# Observability

Use Vercel request telemetry and optional Analytics/Speed Insights only after privacy review. A server-only error-tracking DSN may receive redacted structured events. Propagate correlation IDs through API, Auth, database, provider, webhook, health, background-job, and audit events. Alert on authentication abuse, cross-tenant denials, provider latency/failure, webhook backlog, reconciliation mismatches, and privileged changes.

No external observability service was configured or remotely tested in this task.
